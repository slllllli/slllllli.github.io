The Paper Room is a personal notebook for **close readings of foundational machine-learning papers**. It is written by [slllllli](https://github.com/slllllli) and published in English and Chinese.

## What this is for

Most of us meet important papers through abstracts, threads, and slide decks. Those formats are useful for knowing *that* a result exists. They are much less useful for understanding *why* it was hard, *which* design choice did the work, and *where* the argument stops being true.

This notebook is deliberately slower. Each note tries to reconstruct a paper's argument from the inside:

- the bottleneck the authors were actually facing;
- the mechanism, including the equations and tensor shapes;
- the experiments that carry the claim, and the ablations that isolate it;
- the limits, the later repairs, and the questions that remain open.

## What it is not

It is not a substitute for reading the paper. It is not a news feed, a benchmark leaderboard, or a claim of neutrality. I write these notes to understand the material better, and I will occasionally be wrong. Corrections are welcome.

## A note on language

English is the default edition. Every note has a paired Chinese edition under `/zh/`; use the **EN / 中文** switch in the header to move between them. The two versions are meant to be read as the same argument, not as separate blogs.

## Elsewhere

The source for this site lives at [github.com/slllllli/slllllli.github.io](https://github.com/slllllli/slllllli.github.io). If a note contains an error, or if there is a paper you would like to see analysed, open an issue there.
